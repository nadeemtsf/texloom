\subsection{Threats to interpretation}
\textbf{Construct validity.} Pixel/geometry agreement is not a complete
document-semantics, accessibility or annotation specification. A source hash
checks bytes, not whether the allowed change expressed the user's intention.
Reflow text multiplicities are weaker than unchanged appearance. Success on
one operation family cannot establish another's contract.

\textbf{Internal validity.} Planner, movement package, renderer, prediction or
gold-source preparation can share mistakes. PDFium prediction/inspection
agreement is not two independent formal oracles. A different renderer helps
but can share fonts, input assumptions and the same preparer. The initial
native-entry gold reference actually changed an unrelated line; preserving and
diagnosing that failure is necessary evidence, not an inconvenience to remove.

\textbf{Selection and sampling bias.} Development fixtures influenced the
implementation and diagnostic choices. Reused cases after a reference fix are
not independent replications. Future procedural holdouts still need reviewed
labels and do not represent every class/package or real author workflow.
Clustered analysis must respect repeated requests and engine variants within
documents. No current pilot supports a population-level rare-error bound.

\textbf{External validity.} Results from one Windows host and selected engines/
fonts do not establish universal compilation, pixel identity or latency.
The source-intent pilot's Hebrew/Xe\LaTeX\ condition does not resolve the
missing-package Hebrew/Lua\LaTeX\ setup. Its eight intended cases and reused
fault controls bypass actual visual selection/approval and do not estimate
application false acceptance or refusal. Even exact rendered-reference agreement
cannot distinguish the demonstrated equal-paragraph deletion origins.
The native source-draft transport addresses that exact source identity at a
different entry boundary. Its handles do not prove which printed occurrence
the user selected, and session-local retry receipts cannot resolve every
crash/reopen ambiguity. A source-only diagnostic cannot substitute for the
missing complete visual-operation path.
Current multilingual neighbor cases do not certify every RTL target. Exact
cross-machine appearance can fail for environmental reasons; classify them
instead of quietly relaxing a frozen tolerance.

\textbf{Human factors.} Scripted or simulated interaction does not show that a
new user understands source-unit expansion, a pending outline, a refusal or
the difference between native commit and Save. Actual held gestures and display
changes are different from unit event injection and zoom tests.

\textbf{Review scope.} The literature review is selective and some primary
material remains unretrieved. The report does not infer novelty from a missing
description, or pretend that cryptographic artifact provenance is a complete
security review.

\subsection{Application gates still open}
The owner has reported the named r11/r12 navigation checklists passed,
including held-drag cancellation in the r11 packet. Those observations do not
establish every Escape/Alt+Tab/lost-capture interleaving, Windows DPI/multiple-
monitor transitions, clean-account missing-dependency setup, installation/
uninstall, controlled performance or unassisted usability. These remaining
gates need their own version-specific evidence. Newly implemented source-draft
transport also requires separate integration/input acceptance before it can
become a visual authoring feature.
The earlier uninstaller launch was blocked by the desktop tool and was not
bypassed. Existing-account installer results are not clean-machine results.
No new keyboard language, publication or submission is authorized by this report.

The interface/controller version at \code{5e59e77} passed recorded automated
checks. Later changes have resumed engineering checks and separate named artifacts;
old totals are not promoted to those artifacts. The preceding engineering
section records the newer scope. Diagnostic boundary and overhead measurements
still need their own Windows evidence.
Performance targets require native boundary/overhead validation and a controlled
reference workload. Broader package/paint/shaping reliability and dependency
review remain scoped work; successful builds do not close those gates.
The subsequently identified PDFium advisory is an explicit public-distribution
blocker for the historical pin. A checked development replacement and bounded
runtime checks now exist; complete nested provenance/security review and
replacement native/release acceptance remain explicit gates.

Further source-only guards keep delayed compile requests tied to their
originating client/cancellation generation and recheck compilation permission
after synchronization. Ordinary compile/export cleanup, latest-request search
navigation and pointer ownership have also changed. Their intended behavior
has controller/infrastructure regression evidence, with narrower actual native
observations recorded separately. The archive reader and distributor statement
verification have also been exercised. These engineering checks are not a
fixed-version held-out study and do not replace missing physical/environment
or unassisted usability observations.

\subsection{Next experiments worth doing}
The next correctness experiment should connect natural PDF selection and the
complete interaction path to independent intent/source/render labels. Include
repeated/ambiguous evidence, valid refusals and incorrect acceptance. Refine
the protocol in development; freeze final inputs and labels only after core
stability and review.

The next performance experiment should pair ordinary and diagnostic builds
at actual first/cached-selection and verified-result endpoints. Attribute
excessive cost or refusal to measured source ambiguity, identity work,
compilation, rendering or presentation. Require regression and independent
evidence before changing caching, source support or verification.

Any later paper requires a sharper contribution relative to prior work,
reviewed frozen artifacts and labels, substantive comparisons and a useful
finding. Poor coverage or excessive cost can be informative when measured
and scoped. Publication readiness has not been established; this remains a
technical report draft.
