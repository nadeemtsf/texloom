\section{Evaluation definitions and final-study design}
\label{sec:evaluation}
\pending\ Development fixtures influenced implementation. Their diagnostic
value does not make them a held-out estimate of ordinary use. The final study
will use a recorded application version, sealed inputs/oracles and a protocol
that separates natural interaction from injected-candidate diagnostics.

\subsection{Validity, declared support and execution}
Each intended request needs independent labels for semantic validity and
membership in the declared supported subset. A meaningful request can be
unsupported and safely refused while still representing lost useful coverage.
Support must be declared before running the product, not assigned after a
failure to improve the acceptance rate.

An unsuitable engine or missing package/font can prevent execution of an
otherwise meaningful request. Those availability failures are separate from
deliberate safety refusals. Arabic/Hebrew configuration failures under
pdfLaTeX must not be pooled with supported XeLaTeX/LuaLaTeX workflows. A request
whose intention or oracle is unresolved keeps that status; the product's own
acceptance/refusal message cannot supply its ground truth.

\subsection{Independent ground truth and preservation}
Before product execution, record the intended visual target and exact source
occurrence, allowed source/support-file changes, expected displacement or
reflow, and the independent checking method. Manually prepared expected sources
are compiled outside app caches. A separately pinned renderer and source
annotations check output and bytes; difficult disagreements require explicit
adjudication rather than convenient pairing of repeated text.

The current prototype reference uses Poppler word bounds and full-page RGB
rasters, while the runtime uses PDF.js and PDFium. This reduces one kind of
implementation sharing but does not make reference preparation independent
of the developer. Shared TeX/fonts, an incorrect gold source or an incomplete
task specification can create correlated errors. The initial native-entry
reference failure in Section~\ref{sec:development} is a concrete example.

Source preservation includes the complete enumerated file set, exact bytes
outside allowed changes, permitted support-package insertion, and exact
history/durability checks. BOMs, CRLF/LF, comments and supporting files are not
discarded because the PDF happens to look unchanged. Rendered preservation
uses the policy appropriate to the operation; reflow cases cannot claim the
movement policy's unchanged appearance. Neither comparison alone proves TeX
semantics or the user's intent.

\subsection{Outcomes and denominators}
Let $T$ be the labelled requests in one declared study arm, $V\subseteq T$
the valid intentions, $D\subseteq T$ the declared supported subset and
$U\subseteq T$ the unsafe requests or deliberately incorrect candidates for
that arm. Let $A\subseteq T$ be requests whose canonical source/history was
accepted. Publication, persistence and successful presentation are separately
recorded outcomes. A post-commit error does not erase membership in $A$.

For each accepted request, $\operatorname{Bad}(t)$ means a confirmed violation
of required target, source, preservation or concurrency postconditions;
$\operatorname{All}(t)$ means every required postcondition is established.
The primary reported quantities are
\[
\frac{|\{t\in A:\operatorname{Bad}(t)\}|}{|A|},\qquad
\frac{|A\cap U|}{|U|},\qquad
\frac{|\{t\in A\cap V:\operatorname{All}(t)\}|}{|V|}.
\]
The first is the confirmed incorrect-acceptance fraction among accepted
requests. The second is unsafe acceptance in an independently labelled unsafe
arm, not a substitute for the first. The third is useful correct acceptance
among valid intentions. Report raw counts and the corresponding in-scope
quantity restricted to $V\cap D$.

Valid refusal is $|\{t\in V:\text{decision is refusal}\}|/|V|$, with mapping,
grammar, verifier, budget and other reasons kept distinct. Compile failures,
crashes, timeouts and unresolved outcomes have separate counts and remain in
the availability/useful-success accounting. Consequently correct acceptance
and valid refusal need not sum to one. Do not silently remove failed attempts
from an appealing denominator.

Zero denominators are not applicable, not zero. In particular, deny-all has
no accepted-candidate error fraction and zero useful valid acceptance. If
$I$ accepted requests are confirmed incorrect and $Q$ accepted requests remain
unresolved, report the deterministic adjudication interval
\[
 I/|A|\ \leq\ \text{possible incorrect-acceptance fraction}\ \leq\ (I+Q)/|A|.
\]
This is not a statistical confidence interval. Any population inference needs
an explicit sampling/independence argument, which the current purposive
development controls do not provide.

\subsection{Cases and separation from development}
The proposed minimum is 120 labelled requests over at least 36 new synthetic
documents: at least 60 valid intended edits and 60 unsafe/adversarial candidates.
Exact counts and the allocation to arms are sealed before the first final run.
Report documents, requests, engine variants and timing repetitions separately;
30 timings of one request do not create 30 new correctness cases.

Required strata include ordinary signatures, logical paragraphs and formatting;
repeated strings and repeated macro use; ambiguous/coincident, cross-column
and cross-file evidence; English/Arabic/Hebrew shaping and combining cases;
unsupported tables/math/links/paint/page coordinates; and compilation failure,
staleness, cancellation and corrupted evidence. Development cases stay in
their current directories. New combinations and independently reviewed labels
should be prepared after core stabilization. A set prepared and evaluated by
the same developer is a procedural holdout, not a blinded independent study.

Natural requests and injected packets must never be pooled into one rate.
Record the entry layer: PDF selection, source evidence, proposed source,
compiled candidate or comparator. For example, the native-entry pilot's
refusals before compilation establish a source-plan check, not renderer
sensitivity or the frequency of mistakes in ordinary interaction.

\subsection{Criteria recorded before final execution}
The following are proposed engineering gates, not achieved outcomes or
externally registered thresholds. Amendments must be dated before sealing.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Safety:} zero independently adjudicated incorrect acceptances,
wrong-occurrence commits, stale commits or unrelated-source corruptions in
the sealed suite. Any observed instance fails the finite gate and is retained.
This does not prove zero risk outside the suite.
\item \textbf{Useful coverage:} at least 90\% correct acceptance among
predeclared in-scope valid displacement requests, with per-family/engine
counts. Report broader valid refusals separately rather than hiding them in
a safety total.
\item \textbf{Preservation:} exact bytes outside the allowed patch/file set,
exact history/durability checks, and independent target/unrelated-output
criteria fixed in advance. Runtime tolerances are not permission to tune the
final oracle after seeing results.
\item \textbf{Performance:} the proposed reference-machine goals are p95 source
feedback and prepared selection within 50 ms, a 16.7 ms gesture-frame budget
on a documented 60 Hz setup, and p95 warm small-document verified result within
3 s. The required endpoint and overhead measurements are still pending.
Pointer motion should start no builds; one release should request one logical
operation. TeX passes and private identity builds are counted separately.
\item \textbf{Native reliability:} the exact frozen application must pass its
signature/source/save/recovery workflow, actual held-gesture cancellation,
DPI and installed-artifact checks. Automated/headless results cannot fill
unexecuted Windows cells.
\end{enumerate}
